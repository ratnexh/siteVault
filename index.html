💡 How to verify storage protection in DevTools

You can independently verify browser protection right in DevTools:

  1. Press F12 (or Right-Click → Inspect).
  2. Go to Application tab → Storage.
  3. Look for Persistent storage: Chrome/Edge displays ✓ Persistent storage granted by the browser engine.
